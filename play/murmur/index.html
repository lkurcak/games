      How To Play

      Find Words

      Use the six letters to make English words.

      Letters can repeat. Words need at least four letters.

      Tap letters to build a word, then submit it.

      Hints At 50%

      After you find half the answers, each letter shows how many remaining answers start with it.

      The progress button shows totals by word length.

      Solved

      You cleared the board.

      Every answer found. Nicely done.

      Answers Revealed

      Remaining answers shown.

      Missed words are marked red.
